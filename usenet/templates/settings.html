The upstream network this account resells. Article numbers are handed out by the backbone, not by your provider — two accounts on the same backbone see the same numbers for the same articles, while different backbones agree on nothing numeric.
Name it only when you are sure: providers sharing a backbone then share crawl progress (the second account adds connections, not extra content). Leave it blank and this server is treated as its own backbone, which is the safe assumption — wrongly marking two servers as the same backbone makes each skip articles the other fetched.
See PROVIDERS.md. A picker of known providers and their backbones is planned.

Providers

Active providers are crawled every pass. Backups stay idle and are promoted only to cover an active provider that is failing. Newsgroups are split across providers and across crawler hosts, so adding a provider on a different backbone adds content, while a second account on the same backbone adds connections. See PROVIDERS.md.
